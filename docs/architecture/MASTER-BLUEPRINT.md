# REMRADAR Master Blueprint

## Vision

Build Africa's premier music discovery and cultural intelligence platform through world-class editorial, charts, artist storytelling, music discovery, and immersive digital experiences.

---

# Core Mission

Every visitor should discover something new within 30 seconds.

---

1. Editorial
2. Discovery
3. Charts
4. Artists
5. Music
6. Magazine
7. Events
8. Community

# Homepage Goal

The homepage should feel alive.

It should answer:

- What's happening today?
- Who's trending?
- What should I listen to?
- What should I read?
- What events are coming?
- What's next?
