# REM Brand Identity

## Overview

REM is an African media, technology and creative organisation building premium digital experiences that celebrate music, entertainment, culture and creativity.

Through its ecosystem of products and initiatives, REM empowers creators, connects audiences and develops sustainable platforms that shape the future of African entertainment.

---

To become Africa's leading cultural technology ecosystem connecting creators, audiences and communities through innovative digital experiences.

---

# Mission

To build world-class products, platforms and experiences that celebrate African creativity while empowering artists, businesses and communities.

# RADARCharts by REM

## Overview

RADARCharts by REM is the flagship product within the REM ecosystem.

It is a premium editorial and cultural intelligence platform focused on African music, entertainment, artists, creativity and discovery.

RADARCharts combines journalism, data, storytelling and technology to become the destination where audiences discover what matters before everyone else.

---

## Product Position

A cultural intelligence ecosystem focused on African music, artists, entertainment, culture and discovery.

---

## Product Personality

---

# Design Philosophy

Every REM experience should:

- Feel premium before it feels complex.
- Prioritise storytelling over decoration.
- Guide discovery naturally.
- Reward exploration.
- Maintain consistency across every platform.
- Balance editorial elegance with technical excellence.
- Be memorable through simplicity rather than excess.

---

# Brand Governance

Every current and future REM product must:

- Follow the REM Design System.
- Use approved typography, colours and iconography.
- Maintain consistent editorial quality.
- Follow accessibility standards.
- Preserve a unified visual identity across the ecosystem.

---

# Future Growth

The REM ecosystem is designed to expand over time.

New products, initiatives and platforms should integrate into the existing architecture without compromising consistency, scalability or brand identity.

This document serves as the primary reference for the identity of REM and RADARCharts by REM and should be updated whenever the official brand strategy evolves.
