# Colour System

## Purpose

Defines the official colour palette used throughout the REM ecosystem.

---

# Primary Colours

## REM Blue

Primary interactive colour.

Usage:

- Buttons
- Links
- Highlights
- Icons

---

## White

Primary background colour.

---

## Black

Primary text colour.

---

# Secondary Colours

Accent colours may be introduced for campaigns but should never replace the primary palette.

---

# Functional Colours

## Success

Used for confirmations.

## Warning

Used for caution.

## Error

Used for validation errors.

## Information

Used for notices.

---

# Accessibility

All colours must meet WCAG AA contrast requirements.

Avoid low-contrast combinations.

---

# Usage Rules

Do not introduce unofficial colours.

Maintain consistency across products.

Gradients should remain subtle.

Dark mode should use dedicated colour tokens rather than inverted colours.
