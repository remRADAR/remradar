# Typography

## Purpose

Defines the typography system used throughout the REM ecosystem.

---

# Primary Typeface

Telepaq

Usage:

- Hero headings
- Landing pages
- Marketing campaigns

---

# Secondary Typeface

Frankfurt

Usage:

- Section headings
- Cards
- Editorial titles

---

# Body Typeface

Google Sans

Usage:

- Paragraphs
- Navigation
- Forms
- Buttons
- Labels

---

# Hierarchy

H1

Primary page heading.

H2

Major sections.

H3

Subsections.

Body

General reading.

Caption

Supporting information.

---

# Principles

Maintain generous spacing.

Avoid excessive font weights.

Ensure readability.

Prioritise accessibility.

Keep typography consistent across every page.
