# Brand Voice

## Purpose

Defines how REM communicates with audiences.

---

# Personality

Our voice is:

- Professional
- Confident
- Friendly
- Inspiring
- Authentic
- Modern
- Human

---

# Editorial Style

Write using British English.

Use sentence case.

Avoid sensationalism.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.

Respect artists and creators.

Prioritise clarity over complexity.

---

# Tone

News

Objective.

Editorial

Thoughtful.

Announcements

Exciting.

Community

Inclusive.

Educational

Helpful.

---

# Writing Principles

Write for humans first.

Be accurate.

Be transparent.

Never publish misleading information.

Maintain credibility.

Keep sentences concise.

Use meaningful headings.

---

# Inclusive Language

Respect cultures.

Respect identities.

Avoid stereotypes.

Represent African creativity positively.

---

# Examples

Good:

"Afrobeats continues to influence audiences around the world."

Avoid:

"Everyone agrees Afrobeats is the greatest genre."

---

# Goal

Every piece of communication should strengthen trust in the REM ecosystem.
